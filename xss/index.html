Stay Protected

Top 8 Awareness Rules

Simple habits that stop 95% of attacks.

🔐

Use Strong Passwords

Minimum 12 characters. Mix letters, numbers, symbols. Never reuse across sites.

📱

Enable MFA Everywhere

Multi-factor authentication blocks 99% of automated attacks. Turn it on for every account.

🎣

Spot Phishing

Check sender addresses. Hover over links. Never click urgent "verify now" emails.

🔄

Update Everything

Enable auto-updates. Patches close known vulnerabilities that attackers target.

💾

Backup 3-2-1

3 copies, 2 formats, 1 offsite. Backups defeat ransomware.

📶

Avoid Public WiFi

Use a VPN or your mobile hotspot. Public WiFi = public network.

👀

Least Privilege

Only grant access users actually need. Review permissions regularly.

🚨

Report Immediately

Suspicious activity? Report at once. Fast reporting saves hours of recovery.
